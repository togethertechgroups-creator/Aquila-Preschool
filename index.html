Aquila Montessori Pre-School in Sithalapakkam, Chennai

Nurturing Curious Minds, One Discovery at a Time. Aquila Montessori Pre-School in Sithalapakkam, Chennai offers authentic child-led education for children aged 1.5 to 6. Admissions open for 2025-26. Book a free visit to experience the Montessori method in action.

The Aquila Philosophy: A Nurturing Space to Learn & Grow

AQUILA Montessori Pre - School is a warm and nurturing space where children learn with confidence, curiosity, and creativity through the authentic Montessori approach. We encourage independent, hands-on learning in a joyful child-centered environment that supports holistic growth and lifelong learning in Sithalapakkam, Chennai.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, compassionate, and lifelong learners who grow into responsible individuals with strong values with a love for learning.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, happy, and stimulating Montessori environment where children are encouraged to explore, discover, and develop independence, creativity, and respect for others.

Montessori Programs & Early Childhood Services

Toddler Programme (1.5–2.5 yrs)

Gentle introduction to the Montessori environment. Sensory play, practical life skills, and social bonding in a safe, nurturing space for early childhood learning.

Nursery (2.5–3.5 yrs)

Language development, fine motor activities, cultural exploration, and early mathematical concepts through hands-on Montessori materials.

Junior KG (3.5–4.5 yrs)

Deepening literacy and numeracy through Montessori language materials, sandpaper letters, number rods, and rich storytelling.

Senior KG (4.5–6 yrs)

Preparing children for primary school through advanced Montessori work, collaborative projects, and building strong self-management skills.

BANDA Activity Hub: Engaging After-School Programs in Sithalapakkam

BANDA – the Activity Hub is a forerunner in engaging children in activities beyond the classroom having been established in the year 2016. At BANDA, we believe learning goes beyond the classroom. We offer engaging and skill-based after-school activities for children above 3 years in a fun, safe and encouraging environment.

Classes Offered

Phonics, Comprehension & reading skills, Drawing, Abacus, Art & Craft, Piano, Handwriting, Spoken English, and Summer Camp Activities. Scheduled during evenings and Saturdays.

Preschool Admissions 2025–2026 Process

Admissions are open for the academic year 2025-26. Our simple admissions process includes booking a visit, school tour, meeting the educators, filling the registration form, and confirming enrollment.
